HCDD 331 · Craigslist redesign · Hi-fi prototype v1

Old Craig, organized.

A hybrid of our three lo-fi options: the tab-bar hub from Option C, the search box and filter chips from Option A, and the required listing fields and compare view from Option B. Every task below is fully clickable. Start one to set the persona's city and jump to the first screen.

Find a desk

Sarah · Buyer · Seattle · on her lunch break
  1. Open the "desk" saved search on Home
  2. Change a filter (price, distance or condition)
  3. Tick two desks and compare them
  4. Open a listing and read its key facts
  5. Ask the seller "Can I pick up today?"

Compare hybrid jobs

Mary · Job seeker · Denver
  1. Tap Jobs on Home
  2. Adjust the pay or work-style filters
  3. Tick two or three jobs and compare them
  4. Save a job to come back to later

Sell a dresser

Matt · First-time seller · Austin
  1. Tap Sell and add photos from the camera roll
  2. Type a title and check the suggested category
  3. Fill in the required details
  4. Preview the ad and post it
  5. In Me, mark the old desk as sold
